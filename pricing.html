Questions

What platforms does Marginal run on?

macOS 12 or later, Windows 10 or later, and Linux on x86_64 or aarch64. It is the same app on all three, with a few differences that follow each platform's conventions; the manual's platforms page lists them.

How does the trial start?

Install Marginal and sign in from Settings ▸ Account with Google or GitHub. The first sign-in starts a 14-day trial of the full app. No card is asked for, and nothing is charged when the trial ends; the editor simply becomes read-only until you subscribe.

Is there a free plan?

No, but reading is free. Opening files, the preview, printing, and PDF and HTML export work without a subscription, signed in or not. The subscription is what makes the editor writable.

What happens if I stop paying?

Marginal turns read-only. Your files stay where they are, everything that reads keeps working, and a document you changed while you were licensed can still be saved. Nothing is ever locked.

Does the AI cost extra?

The AI features run on your own API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, or Alibaba Cloud, and the vendor bills usage to that key. Marginal's subscription never gates the AI, and the key is stored in your system keychain, not on Marginal's servers.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Settings ▸ Account ▸ Manage subscription… opens the Stripe customer portal, where you can switch between monthly and yearly, update the card, download invoices, and cancel.

Do I need to be online?

Only to sign in and to subscribe. After that the licence is checked once a day in the background, and a network failure changes nothing; you can keep working offline.

What if a payment fails?

The status shows Payment issue and editing keeps working for a few days while you update the card. After that the app is read-only until the payment goes through.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on each machine. Settings ▸ Account lists the devices signed in, lets you rename them, and lets you deactivate one you no longer use.

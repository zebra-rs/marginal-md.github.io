Updates, built in

Marginal checks for updates and installs them in place. Every update artifact carries a minisign signature that is verified before anything is applied.

Trial and licensing

14 days with the full app, no card. After that, a subscription ($10/mo or $96/yr) keeps the editor writable; if it ever lapses, Marginal turns read-only — reading, preview, PDF export, and saving what you had already changed keep working. AI uses your own Anthropic key and is never locked.

Verified builds

macOS builds are signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. All platforms verify update signatures before installing.

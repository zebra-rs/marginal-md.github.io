Marginal updates itself, but only when you say so.

## Finding out

With **Settings ▸ General ▸ Updates ▸ Check for updates at launch** on, as
it is by default, Marginal looks for a new version a few seconds after it
starts. If there is one, the update dialog opens with the release notes,
and an **Update to …** chip appears in the status bar. **Not Now** closes
the dialog; the chip stays, and clicking it brings the dialog back. Nothing
is downloaded at this point, and a failed check says nothing at all.

**Check for Updates…** in the app menu looks any time. It reports either
*You're up to date* or the new version with its release notes.

## Installing

Click the status bar chip or **Install Update** in the dialog. The update
downloads with a progress bar, installs, and then asks you to **Restart
Now** or **Later**. Keep the app open until the download finishes.

Every update is signed, and the signature is verified before anything is
applied, on all platforms.
